What the models do

Convolutional autoencoder · ae-v1

A small encoder/decoder learns to reconstruct healthy bottle images. Reconstruction residuals are averaged into a 12 × 12 map. Unusual pixels may yield greater error, but reconstruction is not proof of a defect.

Pretrained visual baseline · feature-v1

Frozen first four feature blocks of ImageNet-pretrained MobileNetV3 Small. Healthy training images fit a mean and diagonal standard deviation at each feature position. Mean squared standardized feature distances produce the anomaly map. This is a simple spatial Gaussian baseline, not PatchCore or a state-of-the-art system.

Scoring, thresholds and heatmap scale

The image score averages the largest eight values of a 12 × 12 response map. Each model's threshold is the 95th percentile of held-out healthy-validation image scores. The heatmap uses the 99th percentile of healthy-validation pixel responses as a fixed scale. Ratios are score divided by threshold, not probabilities. Display saturation does not imply certainty.

Preprocessing and scope

Images are decoded as RGB, oriented, stretched to 96 × 96 and center-sampled with nearest-neighbor resizing. The original benchmark uses aligned square bottle views. Phone images, different products, lighting, poses and backgrounds may be far outside that distribution. Uploads are resized in the browser before transfer. Gallery examples use preprocessed PNGs to preserve benchmark inputs.

Serving and latency

ONNX Runtime executes both trained/exported CNNs in a Node API on Vercel. There is no external AI API. Reported server time includes preprocessing and may include cold-start model loading; it excludes network time. No load-test or production throughput claim is made.

Evaluation limits

Results apply only to this bottle-category experiment at 96 × 96 resolution. Small defects may disappear. Pixel-level segmentation quality is not measured. No real production images, independent-site evaluation or industrial safety assessment is included. Small healthy-validation and test sets make false-alarm estimates uncertain.

Stored inspections and privacy

The server processes uploaded images for the current request and does not save them. A normalized thumbnail, model response and review trail can be saved in localStorage in this browser. Hosting infrastructure may retain request metadata. Do not upload private, identifying or confidential images to this public demo. Local review labels are not used to retrain the models.
